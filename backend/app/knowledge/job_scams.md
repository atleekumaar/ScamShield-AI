# Employment Fraud and Job Recruitment Scams

## Overview
Recruitment and job scams exploit job seekers by advertising bogus remote roles, data entry gigs, or freelance positions to collect personal documentation for identity theft or solicit upfront fees.

## Hallmarks of Job Fraud
1. **Unrealistic Compensation vs. Effort**: Offering exorbitant hourly or daily compensation for minimal effort (e.g., "$500/day for liking YouTube videos or rating hotels").
2. **Upfront Equipment or Training Fees**: Urging candidates to purchase proprietary laptops, software licenses, training modules, or background checks before commencing work.
3. **Informal Hiring Processes**: Instant job offers extended solely over WhatsApp, Telegram, or SMS without formal interviews, verifiable HR contracts, or legitimate enterprise email communications.
4. **Task-Based Investment Traps**: Directing recruits to complete small prepaid tasks on customized platforms, gradually demanding substantial financial deposits to release accrued earnings.

## Defensive Mitigations
- Legitimate employers do not request candidate payments for onboarding, hardware equipment, or interview processing.
- Verify job openings directly on the hiring company's official corporate career portal.
- Reject communications originating exclusively from freemail accounts (e.g., `@gmail.com`, `@yahoo.com`) pretending to represent multinational enterprises.
