# Impersonation and Brand Masquerading

## Overview
Impersonation scams occur when bad actors counterfeit the identity of recognized institutions, trusted brands, government officials, or personal acquaintances to establish fraudulent rapport and lower suspicion.

## Common Targets and Masquerading Archetypes
1. **Financial Institutions & Banks**: Pretending to be fraud response desks, credit card departments, or account management teams (e.g., SBI, HDFC, Chase, PayPal).
2. **Government & Tax Authorities**: Impersonating law enforcement, Income Tax departments, Customs agencies, or municipal court systems.
3. **Logistics & Couriers**: Masquerading as FedEx, DHL, USPS, or India Post requesting small fees to release pending parcels.
4. **Tech Support & IT Administrators**: Pretending to be Microsoft, Apple, Google, or internal IT departments claiming device infection.
5. **Executive & Colleague Fraud (BEC)**: Pretending to be a company CEO or manager demanding prompt wire transfers or gift cards.

## Defensive Mitigations
- Corroborate sender contact details against official public records.
- Recognize that legitimate government and banking organizations rarely solicit critical actions or payments through casual SMS, WhatsApp, or unregistered emails.
- Check security certificates and exact domain spellings before engaging.
