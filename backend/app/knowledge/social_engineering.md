# Social Engineering Techniques and Behavioral Exploitation

## Overview
Social engineering leverages human psychological vulnerabilities rather than technical software bugs. Attackers manipulate victims into voluntarily surrendering confidential data, approving unauthorized monetary transfers, or granting unauthorized access.

## Primary Psychological Triggers
1. **Urgency & Scarcity**: Creating artificial time constraints ("Expires in 15 minutes", "Only 2 spots left") to bypass cognitive rationalization.
2. **Fear & Coercion**: Fabricating severe consequences such as arrest warrants, IRS/police investigations, utility shutoffs, or account freezes.
3. **Authority Pressure**: Pretending to be executive leadership, regulatory bodies, judicial authorities, or law enforcement officers.
4. **Sympathy & Emotional Appeal**: Fabricating humanitarian crises, injured family members, or emergency bail requests.
5. **Greed & False Reciprocity**: Promises of extraordinary investment returns, lottery payouts, or sudden grants.

## Defensive Mitigations
- Enforce the "Stop, Think, Verify" protocol whenever an emotional reaction or urgency is induced.
- Establish an independent communication channel to corroborate unusual requests.
- Never authorize transactions or divulge secrets based on telephone or chat demands.
