# Financial Scams and Payment Fraud Schemes

## Overview
Financial fraud encompasses scams orchestrated to misappropriate victim funds directly via unauthorized electronic transfers, UPI manipulation, cryptocurrency deposits, or advance-fee loan and investment frauds.

## Prevalent Patterns
1. **UPI / Payment App Exploits**: Deceptive QR codes or "Collect Request" links where the victim is promised an incoming refund or lottery prize, but entering their PIN actually debits their account.
2. **High-Yield Investment & Ponzi Schemes ("Pig Butchering")**: Fabricated crypto investment portals demonstrating fictitious rapid gains, requiring ongoing deposits to unlock withdrawals.
3. **Advance Fee & Fake Refund Scams**: Demanding processing fees, customs release charges, or security deposits prior to releasing fictitious grants, refunds, or lottery winnings.
4. **Card / Banking Account Freeze Hoaxes**: Coercing victims to move savings into "safe accounts" or temporary holding accounts under criminal investigation.

## Defensive Mitigations
- Entering a UPI PIN or banking PIN is solely required to send money or check balance, NEVER to receive funds.
- Disregard unsolicited investment recommendations, guaranteed return promises, or offshore crypto trading tips.
- Maintain independent verification with financial institutions using official telephone directories.
